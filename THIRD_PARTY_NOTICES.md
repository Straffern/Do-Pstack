## pstack

Source: [cursor/plugins `pstack/`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack), by Lauren Tan.

This package includes these parts of pstack, some adapted for omp, Pi, and Claude Code:

- `skills/`, from `pstack/skills`. Directories and YAML names are renamed to `do-*`. `upstream.lock.json` records the upstream version each file is based on.
- `agents/poteto-agent.md` and `agents/comment-sicko.md`, from `pstack/agents`.
- `automations/benny/`, from `pstack/automations/benny`.
- `docs/guide/`, from `pstack/docs/guide`, including its images.
